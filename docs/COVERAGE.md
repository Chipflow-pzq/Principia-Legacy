# Coverage: records, explanations, and gaps

The [current build status](../BUILD_STATUS.md) gives the actual subject totals. The [snapshot-pinned coverage report](../reports/coverage-1.5.json) lists every topic, its endpoint, selected route state and unresolved gaps; the [catalogue report](../reports/catalog-1.5.json) describes works and individual profiles.

| Measure | What it establishes |
|---|---|
| Registered topic | An identified scope in the catalogue; it may have no extracted insights. |
| Topic with content | At least one visible insight; this is not the 1.5 populated-topic milestone. |
| Eight or more insights | Sufficient depth to consider a route, without certifying its coherence. |
| Ready reading route | At least eight substantive insights in a connected, exact-revision core; resolved endpoint, relevant foundations, complete joint premises, explicit limits and gaps, and positive machine editorial assessment. |
| Work with extracted insights | At least one admitted insight linked to that sourced work. |
| Insight count | Unique visible contributions, counted once even when shared by several topics or works. |

The 1.5 target is **100 ready topics and 1,000 substantive insights per subject**. The longer-term target remains 200 topics and 2,000 insights. Shortfalls are explicit. A connected component, catalogue size or positive model verdict alone does not satisfy the route milestone.

Historical influence, logical dependence, evidential support, analogy and teaching links retain separate meanings. A shared topic does not justify an arrow. An exposition does not transfer original credit to its author. Independent discoveries remain distinct; unsupported candidates and recheck-required interpretations stay outside ordinary browsing.

All current coverage remains exploratory and machine-screened. **No qualified human audit has been performed.** The separate 100-insight/150-relation sample is a review queue, not a completed audit. Earlier release reports and immutable manifests preserve their original counts and limitations.
